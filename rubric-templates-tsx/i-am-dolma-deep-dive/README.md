### Тексты слайдов 2–6

1. Dolma starts as a pile of grape leaves and filling. The dish only exists after every piece has been rolled by hand.
2. In most Armenian kitchens, dolma is not made alone. It is made in groups, with each person rolling at their own pace.
3. There is no definitive dolma. Grape leaves, cabbage leaves, or stuffed peppers and aubergines — all of them count.
4. Dolma is almost always served with matsun. The cool dairy against the warm filling is not a suggestion. It is the point.
5. After the table is cleared, the grape leaves and bowl and cloth tell you exactly what happened here.
